# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indie hackers, startup founders, and dev teams evaluating AI agent tooling. They arrive from GitHub, Twitter/X, or Hacker News. They're technically sharp, time-poor, and skeptical of vaporware. They want to see a working agent, understand the architecture in seconds, and either fork it or hire someone to build them one.

## Product Purpose

eveonn.xyz is an open-source always-on agent starter built with Vercel Eve. It exists to:
1. Demonstrate what an always-on AI agent looks and feels like (live demo at /chat)
2. Let developers fork, customize, and deploy their own agent in minutes
3. Drive consulting leads to HeySalad for custom agent builds ($2,000+)

Success = forks, stars, inbound leads, and paid custom agent engagements.

## Brand Personality

Agentic. Actionable. Advantageous.

- Speaks like a sharp founder, not a marketer
- Shows capability through demonstration, not claims
- Feels like infrastructure you can trust, not a toy
- Confident without being arrogant

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with cream/sand backgrounds
- Stripe-clone or Linear-clone aesthetics (overused in dev tools)
- Corporate enterprise feel (blue gradients, stock photos, trust badges)
- Overly minimal "coming soon" pages with no substance
- Glassmorphism-heavy designs with blur cards everywhere
- The numbered section eyebrow pattern (01 / 02 / 03)

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — The agent is live. Let people use it, not read about it.
2. **Earn trust through craft** — Every pixel signals "these people know what they're doing."
3. **Action over admiration** — Every section drives toward a concrete next step (try it, fork it, hire us).
4. **Technical credibility** — Speak the audience's language. No marketing fluff.
5. **Always on, always ready** — The design should feel alive, current, and operational.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
- Color-blind safe palette (don't rely on color alone for meaning)
- Keyboard navigable throughout
